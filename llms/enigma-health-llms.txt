# Enigma Health

> Enigma Health (Enigma Global eHealth) is a Netherlands-based digital health
> startup building a secure, private eHealth portal intended to connect patients,
> doctors and health institutions, and to give patients lifelong global access to
> their own electronic health record and digital vaccine passport. The company is
> a Startup level Industry Partner of the openEHR International Foundation.

## Status

As of 2026-09-02, Enigma Health publishes **no public API, no developer program and
no machine-readable contract**. The company website is a pre-launch "Coming Soon"
splash page. Everything below is an honest record of what was probed and what was
found, so an agent does not waste calls looking for a surface that does not exist.

- Website: https://enigma-health.org/ (HTTP 200 — single-page "Coming Soon")
- No developer portal, documentation, API reference, pricing or sign-up page
- No api./docs./developer./app./portal. subdomain (all NXDOMAIN)
- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, gRPC/Protobuf, WSDL, AsyncAPI or MCP endpoint
- No /.well-known/ documents (security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json,
  agent.json all 404)
- No first-party SDK on npm, PyPI or RubyGems
- No published plans, rate limits, changelog, status page or trust center
